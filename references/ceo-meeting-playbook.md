# CEO Meeting Playbook

Use this reference when preparing the user for a CEO, Founder, or Board conversation about overseas growth, Singapore market entry, supplier onboarding, or resources.

## Meeting Goal

The default goal is to win authorization for a dedicated overseas growth pod and a 90-day Singapore pilot.

The user should not try to sound like they already have all answers. The stronger CMO stance is:

`我现在不假装海外市场已经被证明，但我认为新加坡已经有足够信号值得做一个CEO背书的90天试点。我的请求不是大规模投入，而是要一个可以真正把供应商、设计师、产品和本地团队串起来的专职小组。`

English version:

`I am not claiming the overseas model has already been proven. I am saying Singapore has enough signal to justify a CEO-backed 90-day pilot with a dedicated cross-functional pod.`

## Five-Page CEO Pack

### Page 1: Opening Logic

Include:
- Current situation: overseas platform is being pushed, Singapore supplier conference has created initial contact, but the operating model is not yet clear.
- Core diagnosis: supplier onboarding is not only a sales problem; it is a model-validation and organization-priority problem.
- Strategic ask: approve a 90-day Singapore pilot and dedicated overseas growth team.

Suggested script:

`我今天不想给您一个看起来很完整但其实没有验证过的海外市场答案。我想给您一个更可执行的判断：新加坡现在最需要的不是更多口号，而是一个被授权的90天试点，把供应商入驻、设计师使用、平台内容和项目转化真正跑通。`

### Page 2: Singapore Status And Barriers

Separate into:
- Known facts: supplier conference happened; suppliers have been invited; global library is not mature; onboarding is heavy.
- Assumptions: suppliers care about ROI, branch teams may not have KPI, distributors may lack time, early platform traffic is uncertain.
- Decisions needed: who owns overseas growth; how much cross-functional priority exists; what pilot metrics define success.

Frame barriers:
- ROI unclear.
- Onboarding burden too high.
- Local branch/distributor incentive mismatch.
- Platform supply and demand are not yet synchronized.
- Internal product/content support may not be prioritized.

### Page 3: 90-Day Pilot Path

Default phases:
- Days 1-15: confirm supplier list, segment by priority, conduct interviews, define minimum onboarding requirement.
- Days 16-45: onboard first supplier cohort, clean content workflow, align designer usage scenarios.
- Days 46-75: push usage inside Singapore-related projects, track specification and inquiry signals.
- Days 76-90: review conversion, supplier feedback, designer adoption, content workload, and commercial model.

Pilot metrics:
- Number of priority suppliers contacted.
- Supplier interview completion rate.
- Qualified suppliers agreeing to pilot.
- Suppliers completing minimum onboarding.
- Material items live on platform.
- Designer usage or specification events.
- Supplier feedback on lead quality or project visibility.
- Internal cycle time for onboarding.

### Page 4: Resource Request

Minimum team:
- User: overseas growth owner and CEO-facing accountable person.
- Supplier BD/operator: owns supplier recruitment and follow-up.
- Product/platform support: owns onboarding flow issues and data/content constraints.
- Material content support: owns catalog, images, specs, tags, and translation coordination.
- Singapore local coordinator: owns local supplier communication, meeting logistics, and branch alignment.

Ask CEO for:
- Clear authorization that this pilot is company priority.
- Named people or departments assigned to the pod.
- Weekly or biweekly CEO review during the first 90 days.
- Permission to judge the first phase by pilot learning and adoption signals, not immediate revenue.

### Page 5: Decisions For CEO

Ask these directly:
- Do we agree Singapore is the first overseas pilot market?
- Do we agree the goal is to validate the platform model before promising revenue?
- Can I own the 90-day pilot as the accountable lead?
- Can you assign a dedicated cross-functional pod?
- Can you help make overseas branch, product, and design-side usage part of the same priority?

## CEO Objections And Responses

Objection: `Why do we need a dedicated team before revenue is proven?`

Response:
`正因为收入还没被证明，才需要专职小组。没有专职小组，验证动作会被拆散在各部门的边角时间里，最后我们无法判断是市场不成立，还是执行机制没有成立。`

Objection: `Can you just ask suppliers to upload first?`

Response:
`可以要求，但早期供应商不会因为平台存在就投入时间。我们需要先降低入驻成本，并给他们一个清楚的试点收益逻辑：项目曝光、设计师使用、早期合作身份和后续商业机会。`

Objection: `Why Singapore first?`

Response:
`因为新加坡已经有供应商会议和母公司设计业务关系，是最容易把供应商、设计师和平台使用连接起来的试点场景。它不是全球答案，但适合作为第一个验证样板。`

Objection: `What if suppliers still do not join?`

Response:
`那也是重要结论，但前提是我们用正确方式验证过：明确ROI、降低入驻负担、让本地团队有KPI、让设计师端产生真实使用。如果这样仍然推不动，我们才能判断商业模型要调整。`

## Meeting Close

End with a decision request:

`我希望今天拿到的不是一个泛泛的支持，而是三个明确决定：第一，新加坡作为90天试点；第二，我作为负责人；第三，给我一个跨部门专职小组和CEO背书。这样我可以在两周内给您第一版供应商反馈和落地进度。`
