# Supplier Onboarding Growth

Use this reference to turn supplier hesitation into a practical onboarding and persuasion system.

## Core Diagnosis

Early supplier resistance is rational. They are being asked to spend time on a platform that does not yet prove traffic, revenue, or internal priority. The CMO job is to reduce perceived risk, reduce onboarding effort, and create a credible path to value.

## Objection Map

Common objections:
- `What ROI will this bring?`
- `We do not have time to upload materials.`
- `This is not our local branch KPI.`
- `Headquarters controls product information.`
- `We already work through distributors or direct designer relationships.`
- `The platform does not have enough users yet.`

Convert each objection:
- ROI concern -> pilot metric and project exposure story.
- Time concern -> concierge onboarding and minimum data path.
- KPI concern -> CEO/branch-level alignment and early partner status.
- Data concern -> phased content standard.
- Channel conflict -> platform as specification support, not immediate replacement.
- Low traffic concern -> design-company project access and early category ownership.

## Value Proposition

### Supplier Headquarters

Chinese:
`这是一个进入设计师选材流程的长期入口，不只是一次广告投放。早期加入可以帮助品牌在平台品类标准和展示方式上占据先发位置。`

English:
`This is a long-term entry point into the designer specification workflow, not a one-off advertising placement. Early participation gives your brand a first-mover position in category visibility and platform standards.`

### Singapore Branch Or Distributor

Chinese:
`我们不会一开始要求你们投入大量时间。试点阶段可以用最小资料包先上线核心产品，并由我们协助整理内容，目标是让你们更容易被设计项目看到。`

English:
`We are not asking your local team to take on a heavy setup burden upfront. For the pilot, we can start with a minimum product package and support the content preparation so your materials can be discovered by relevant design projects faster.`

### Sales Manager

English:
`The platform can help your products stay visible when designers are comparing materials early in the project, before procurement conversations begin.`

### Marketing Team

English:
`The platform gives your product content a structured place inside the design workflow, with potential visibility into which categories and materials designers are engaging with.`

## Low-Friction Onboarding Path

Split onboarding into phases:

### Minimum Pilot Package

Require only:
- Brand profile.
- Priority category.
- 10-30 hero products or material lines.
- Images or catalog PDFs.
- Basic specifications.
- Contact person.

Avoid asking for complete global catalogues at the start.

### Assisted Setup

Offer:
- Platform team extracts information from existing catalogues.
- Supplier approves cleaned content.
- Local branch only confirms priority products and contact route.
- Translation can be phased.

### Expansion Package

Add later:
- Full catalogue.
- Technical certificates.
- Sustainability documents.
- Pricing logic if appropriate.
- Sample request workflow.
- Project case studies.

## Early-Partner Offer

Use when supplier needs urgency:

- Priority category placement during pilot.
- Early feedback access from designers.
- Co-created product display standards.
- First cohort status in Singapore materials library.
- Opportunity to be included in pilot project recommendation lists.

Do not overpromise direct sales. Promise structured exposure, usage feedback, and a measurable pilot.

## Supplier Follow-Up Sequence

### Message 1: Thank You And Pilot Framing

English:
`Thank you for joining the Singapore supplier session. We are building the first overseas pilot of our materials platform, focused on helping designers discover and specify suitable products more efficiently. For the pilot stage, we would like to start with a lightweight onboarding path rather than a full catalogue upload.`

### Message 2: Reduce Workload

English:
`To reduce the workload for your local team, we can begin with a minimum product package: your priority category, selected hero products, existing images or catalogues, and one contact person for confirmation. Our team can support the content structuring before final approval.`

### Message 3: Explain Value

English:
`The immediate goal is not to ask for a large commitment before ROI is proven. The goal is to place your materials inside the designer selection workflow and track early signals from relevant projects during the Singapore pilot.`

### Message 4: Concrete Ask

English:
`Could we schedule a 30-minute onboarding call this week to identify the first 10-30 products or material lines that should be included in the pilot?`

## Funnel Metrics

Track:
- Suppliers contacted.
- Replies.
- Meetings booked.
- Pilot interest confirmed.
- Minimum product package received.
- Content approved.
- Products live.
- Designer views/searches/specification signals.
- Supplier feedback.
- Drop-off reasons.

Use drop-off reasons to fix the system, not blame the supplier.
