# Team Management Cadence

Use this reference after CEO authorization or when planning how the user should manage downward.

## Minimum Viable Overseas Growth Pod

Default roles:
- CMO owner: accountable for strategy, CEO updates, prioritization, and cross-functional alignment.
- Supplier BD/operator: owns supplier segmentation, outreach, follow-up, and conversion.
- Product/platform support: owns onboarding friction, platform issues, and data workflow constraints.
- Material content support: owns catalog cleanup, images, specs, translation coordination, and publishing.
- Singapore local coordinator: owns local supplier communication, meeting coordination, and branch alignment.

## Role Responsibilities

### CMO Owner

Responsibilities:
- Define weekly objective.
- Decide priority supplier segments.
- Run CEO updates.
- Remove blockers.
- Protect the pilot from becoming scattered side work.

### Supplier BD/Operator

Responsibilities:
- Build supplier tracker.
- Book supplier calls.
- Record objections.
- Move suppliers through funnel stages.
- Report conversion and blockers.

### Product/Platform Support

Responsibilities:
- Define minimum onboarding flow.
- Identify product friction.
- Prioritize quick fixes.
- Track platform readiness.

### Material Content Support

Responsibilities:
- Convert supplier catalogues into platform-ready content.
- Define minimum content standard.
- Manage translation and image/spec requirements.
- Report content cycle time.

### Singapore Local Coordinator

Responsibilities:
- Help with local context.
- Coordinate meetings.
- Clarify distributor or branch decision makers.
- Keep local team aligned with pilot priorities.

## Weekly Cadence

### Monday Growth Standup

Agenda:
- What is this week's single most important outcome?
- Which suppliers must move forward?
- Which blockers need escalation?
- What must be ready for designers or platform usage?

### Midweek Blocker Review

Agenda:
- Supplier drop-offs.
- Product/content bottlenecks.
- Local branch coordination issues.
- Decisions needed from user or CEO.

### Friday CEO-Ready Update

Format:
- This week's progress.
- What we learned.
- Where the funnel is blocked.
- Decisions or help needed.
- Next week's priority.

## KPI Tree

North-star pilot question:
`Can Singapore validate a repeatable overseas materials platform model?`

Pilot KPIs:
- Supplier activation.
- Content readiness.
- Designer usage.
- Project/specification signal.
- Internal cycle time.

Individual KPIs:
- BD: supplier meetings, pilot confirmations, content package collection.
- Content: product lines prepared, approval cycle time, live listings.
- Product: friction items solved, onboarding flow readiness.
- Local coordinator: local decision-maker mapping, meeting completion, branch alignment.
- CMO owner: CEO decisions obtained, weekly priority clarity, blocker resolution.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to CEO when:
- A department treats the pilot as optional despite prior agreement.
- Local branch refuses ownership because KPI is unclear.
- Product or content support cannot be assigned.
- Supplier asks for a decision that exceeds team authority.

Do not escalate vague frustration. Escalate with:
- Decision needed.
- Business impact.
- Options.
- Recommended choice.
- Deadline.

## Management Language

Use this with team members:

`这不是一个“大家有空支持一下”的项目。我们要在90天内验证新加坡能不能跑出海外材料平台的样板，所以每个人都需要有明确交付物。你这周的目标不是“跟进供应商”，而是让5家优先供应商完成试点意向确认，并拿到最小资料包。`

English version:

`This is not a side-support project. In 90 days we need to validate whether Singapore can become the first repeatable overseas materials-platform model. Your goal this week is not simply to follow up with suppliers; it is to move five priority suppliers to pilot confirmation and collect their minimum content package.`
